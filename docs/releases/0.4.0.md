## CatalyX 0.4.0

The first tagged release of CatalyX: a lean, ad-free, fully reversible game booster for Windows.

### New in this release
- **One boost control, three looks.** A launch bar along the bottom of the Boost page, a gauge, or a power ring (Settings > Appearance). They share one state and behave identically, and they follow your accent colour.
- **A summary before every boost**: exactly what will change, with a "Don't ask me again" box (Settings > Behaviour > *Ask before boosting*). Restore never asks.
- **Quiet desktop** (Max Squeeze): Windows' taskbar is switched off while a game runs and comes back when it closes, on **Ctrl+Alt+End**, or after a crash.
- **Smaller when minimised**: about 2 MB instead of about 70 MB while a game is running.
- A lighter background watcher, and the old PureBoost settings and scheduled tasks are carried over automatically.

### Download
`catalyx.exe` is a single file: no installer, nothing written outside `%APPDATA%\CatalyX`. Verify it against `catalyx.exe.sha256`.

Windows 10/11. Graphics-card tuning needs an NVIDIA card; a few features need administrator rights. See the [README](https://github.com/TheMR-777/CatalyX#readme) and [docs](https://github.com/TheMR-777/CatalyX/tree/main/docs).
